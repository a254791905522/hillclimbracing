About the Game

Hill Climb Racing is a physics-based driving experience where control is everything. Push forward to gain speed, brake to keep balance, and adapt to the terrain in real time.

Your goal is simple: drive farther, earn more coins, and improve your vehicle to take on harder climbs. The challenge comes from maintaining momentum without flipping over.

Whether you play for quick fun or long improvement sessions, each run gives you a new chance to master the hills and set a better record.
